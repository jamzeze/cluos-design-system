# CluOS Design System 2026.07.29

Status: current

Visual direction: Thermal Nocturne

## Canonical foundations

- Azigos Deep Navy and Medium Blue establish the base.
- Tech Green and Operational Teal are the primary CluOS signals.
- Operational Teal is `#16A39A` on dark surfaces.
- Deep Teal is `#08736E` on light surfaces.
- Blood Copper is restricted to warning and human-review states.
- Oxblood is restricted to error, blocking, and destructive states.
- Manrope and Instrument Sans form the approved typography pairing.
- The eight approved motion behaviors remain part of the current system.
